The Artist

Patience, layered in graphite.

I'm Vishal Trivedi — an artist working mostly in graphite and charcoal, with paint when a subject asks for colour. I'm drawn to texture: the grain of paper, the weight of a shadow, the slow build of tone that turns a few lines into something that feels alive.

Alongside my studies at IIM Bangalore and IIT Madras, drawing is where I slow down. What began as commissions for friends and neighbours has grown into this collection — every piece an original, made by hand.
