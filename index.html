About the Artist

Capturing the unseen details of everyday moments.

Hello, I'm Elena. I am a visual artist based in the pacific northwest, working primarily with watercolor, textured acrylics, and raw charcoal. My work is inspired by the quiet dialogues between light and landscape, and the emotional resonance of empty spaces.

Every piece is handcrafted with archival-grade materials, designed to bring a sense of calm and tactile depth into your space. For sales, inquiries, or custom commission work, feel free to reach out via WhatsApp.
